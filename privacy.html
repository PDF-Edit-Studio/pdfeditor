PDF Edit Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PDF Edit Studio is made by Hokfam Ltd. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: we don't collect any data.

Your documents

PDFs you open, edit, redact, sign, compress, OCR, summarise or translate are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

On-device AI and translation

AI summaries and answers use Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) where your device supports it. Translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework; your system may download language packs from Apple. Document text is not sent to us.

Saved signatures

Signatures you choose to save are encrypted and stored only on your device. The encryption key is kept in your device's Keychain.

No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking

The app has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Purchases

The app is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we receive no payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: email pdfESupport@hokfam.com or see the Support page.
